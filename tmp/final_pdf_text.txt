BEFORE YOU DECIDE
Things they don't always tell you.
You have a date to sign by. Here's what the official rules say, so you can decide on your timeline, not theirs.
Every point links to a government source.
WHAT PEOPLE OFTEN THINK
"Everyone in Ontario gets severance pay on
top of notice."
WHAT THE OFFICIAL RULES SAY
The separate severance pay layer only applies after 5 or more
years of service, and only with a payroll of $2.5 million or more or
50 or more people cut in a closure.
Source: CA-ON-S2
WHAT PEOPLE OFTEN THINK
"A layoff is always temporary."
WHAT THE OFFICIAL RULES SAY
A layoff only stays temporary for a limited time. After that it is a
termination, and if your contract never allowed layoffs, a layoff can
itself be a termination.
Source: CA-ON-S1
WHAT PEOPLE OFTEN THINK
"If one part of a termination clause is wrong,
the rest still counts."
WHAT THE OFFICIAL RULES SAY
In Ontario, if any part of the clause breaks the Employment
Standards Act, the whole clause fails.
Source: CA-ON-S5
WHAT PEOPLE OFTEN THINK
"Taking separation pay delays EI."
WHAT THE OFFICIAL RULES SAY
For claims from March 30, 2025 to October 9, 2027, separation pay
is not deducted and the EI waiting period is waived.
Source: CA-ON-S6

SECTION 2 OF 5 · TWO NUMBERS TO UNDERSTAND
The floor — and the range above it.
One number is a rule. The other is an estimate. Here is how each is worked out.
1 · The legal minimum (a rule you can check)
Termination pay: 3 weeks × weekly pay = $231 (Employment Standards Act)
Severance pay: 14.5 weeks × weekly pay = $346
Vacation pay on termination pay: 6% × termination pay = $1,038
Legal minimum: $577
2 · What a court might award (our estimate)
If your contract doesn't validly limit you, a court can award "reasonable notice" instead of the minimum, based on
four factors: length of service, age, job level and similar jobs nearby. For you: about 3–5 months of base pay,
$2,875–$3,833. Courts rarely go past about 24 months. Pay from a new job usually reduces the part above the
minimum.
